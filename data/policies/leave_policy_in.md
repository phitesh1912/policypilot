# Leave Policy — India

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Acme Corp employed by Acme Technologies India
Private Limited. Leave entitlements are governed by the Karnataka Shops and Commercial
Establishments Act and Acme's internal policy, whichever is more favourable to the employee.

## Leave year and eligibility

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Employees become eligible for all leave types from
their date of joining. Leave is credited on a pro-rata basis in the first year.

## Earned leave (EL)

Employees accrue 1.5 days of earned leave per completed month of service, which is 18 days per
leave year. Earned leave is credited on the first working day of each month.

Unused earned leave can be carried forward to the next leave year up to a maximum balance of
45 days. Any balance above 45 days on 31 March lapses unless encashed.

Earned leave must be applied at least 7 calendar days in advance through the HR portal. Requests
of more than 5 consecutive working days require approval from both the reporting manager and the
department head.

## Casual leave (CL)

Employees receive 7 days of casual leave per leave year, credited in full on 1 April (pro-rated
for new joiners). Casual leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed. A maximum of
3 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at once. Casual leave may be applied on the same
day in case of an emergency, with manager notification by 10:00 AM.

## Sick leave (SL)

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive
days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, submitted within 3
working days of returning to work. Up to 6 days of unused sick leave carry forward to the next
leave year, up to a maximum balance of 24 days.

## Maternity leave

Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave in accordance with the
Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected
date of delivery. For the third and subsequent children, the entitlement is 12 weeks. Employees
adopting a child below the age of three months are entitled to 12 weeks of leave from the date the
child is handed over.

## Paternity leave

Employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of
the birth or adoption of the child. Paternity leave may be split into two blocks.

## Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate
family member (spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law, or sibling).

## Public holidays

Acme India observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 restricted (optional) holidays per calendar
year. The holiday calendar is published by HR each December. Restricted holidays must be applied
for in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance.

## Leave encashment

Earned leave above 30 days may be encashed once per leave year, in January, up to a maximum of
10 days. On separation, the entire earned leave balance is encashed at the employee's last drawn
basic salary. Casual and sick leave are not encashable.

## Leave without pay (LWP)

Once all paid leave is exhausted, an employee may apply for leave without pay with department
head approval. LWP of more than 30 days in a leave year requires approval from the HR Business
Partner and pauses earned leave accrual for the duration.

## Sandwich rule

If leave is taken on the working days immediately before and after a weekend or public holiday,
the intervening weekend or holiday is also counted as leave. This rule does not apply to sick leave
supported by a medical certificate.
