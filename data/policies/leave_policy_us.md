# Leave Policy — United States

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corp Inc. in the
United States. Where state or local law provides a greater benefit, the law applies.

## Leave year

The leave year is the calendar year, 1 January to 31 December.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Acme US uses a combined PTO bank that covers vacation and personal time. PTO accrues each
bi-weekly pay period based on years of service:

| Years of service | Annual PTO | Accrual per pay period |
|------------------|-----------:|-----------------------:|
| 0 – 2 years      | 15 days    | 4.62 hours             |
| 3 – 5 years      | 20 days    | 6.15 hours             |
| 6+ years         | 25 days    | 7.69 hours             |

Part-time employees accrue PTO proportionally to scheduled hours.

Up to 40 hours (5 days) of unused PTO may be carried into the next calendar year. Any additional
balance is forfeited on 31 December, except in states where forfeiture is prohibited (currently
California, Montana, and Nebraska), where the balance continues to accrue up to a cap of 1.5 times
the annual accrual.

PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
Shorter requests should be submitted at least 48 hours in advance where practicable.

## Sick leave

Acme US provides 10 days (80 hours) of paid sick leave per calendar year, front-loaded on
1 January. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments, or to care
for a family member. Unused sick leave carries over to the next year up to a maximum balance of
120 hours. A doctor's note may be requested for absences of more than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Birth parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birth parents and adoptive
parents are entitled to 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must be used within
12 months of the birth or placement and runs concurrently with FMLA leave where applicable.

## Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)

Employees who have worked for Acme for at least 12 months and at least 1,250 hours in the past
12 months are eligible for up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per rolling 12-month
period under FMLA. Employees must use available PTO and sick leave concurrently with FMLA leave.

## Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to 5 business days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate
family member and up to 2 days for an extended family member.

## Jury duty and voting

Acme pays full salary for up to 10 business days of jury duty per year. Employees may take up to
2 hours of paid time to vote if polls are not open for at least 3 hours outside working hours.

## Company holidays

Acme US observes 11 paid company holidays per year, including a floating holiday that may be used
on any day with manager approval. The holiday schedule is published each November.

## PTO payout on separation

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out on separation where required by state law. In all other states,
PTO is paid out only to employees who provide at least 2 weeks' written notice and work through
the notice period. Sick leave is never paid out on separation.

## Unpaid leave

Employees who have exhausted paid leave may request an unpaid personal leave of absence of up to
30 days with approval from their manager and HR Business Partner. Benefits continue during
approved unpaid leave of 30 days or less.
