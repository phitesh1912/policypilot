# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Acme Corp is committed to conducting business with integrity, respect, and in compliance with the
law. This Code applies to all employees, contractors, and board members. Violations may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Respect in the workplace

Acme does not tolerate harassment, bullying, or discrimination on the basis of race, colour,
religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or any other
protected characteristic. All employees must complete anti-harassment training within 30 days of
joining and annually thereafter.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with Acme's interests. Outside
employment, board positions, and financial interests of more than 1% in a competitor, customer, or
supplier must be disclosed to Legal through the conflict-of-interest form in the HR portal.
Employees may not supervise or make employment decisions about a relative or romantic partner.

## Gifts and entertainment

Employees may accept gifts or entertainment from business partners only if the value does not
exceed USD 100 (or INR 5,000) per instance and USD 250 (or INR 15,000) per year from any single
source. Cash and cash equivalents may never be accepted. Gifts to government officials are
prohibited without prior written approval from Legal.

## Confidential information

Employees must protect Acme's confidential information and that of customers and partners. This
includes source code, product roadmaps, financial results, customer lists, and employee personal
data. Confidential information must not be shared outside Acme without a signed non-disclosure
agreement, and must not be entered into external AI tools or services that are not on the approved
tools list maintained by Security.

## Data privacy

Employees who handle personal data must follow the Data Protection Policy, access only data
required for their role, and report any suspected data breach to security@acme.example within
1 hour of discovery. Employee salary, performance, and health information is restricted to HR,
Payroll, and the employee's management chain.

## Use of company resources

Acme equipment, systems, and accounts are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is
permitted provided it does not interfere with work or violate any policy. Acme may monitor company
systems in accordance with local law.

## Insider trading

Employees who possess material non-public information about Acme or its partners may not trade in
the relevant securities or share that information with others. Trading windows and blackout periods
are communicated by Legal each quarter.

## Anti-bribery and corruption

Acme prohibits offering, promising, giving, or accepting bribes or facilitation payments in any
form. This applies to dealings with government officials and private parties alike.

## Social media and public statements

Only authorised spokespeople may speak on behalf of Acme. Employees discussing Acme-related topics
on personal social media must make clear the views are their own and must not disclose confidential
information.

## Speaking up

Employees are encouraged to report suspected violations to their manager, HR, Legal, or through
the anonymous ethics hotline at ethics.acme.example, available 24/7. Acme prohibits retaliation
against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.

## Investigations

Reports are reviewed by the Ethics Committee within 5 business days. Employees are expected to
cooperate fully and truthfully with investigations and to keep investigation details confidential.

## Acknowledgement

All employees must acknowledge the Code of Conduct in the HR portal within 7 days of joining and
each January thereafter.
