# Payroll & Overtime FAQ — India

## When is salary paid?

Salary is paid on the last working day of each month by bank transfer. If the last working day
falls on a bank holiday, salary is paid on the preceding working day. Payslips are available in the
HR portal on the morning of payday.

## What is the salary structure?

A standard Acme India CTC is split into: basic salary (40% of CTC), house rent allowance (50% of
basic), special allowance (the balance), employer provident fund contribution (12% of basic), and
gratuity provision (4.81% of basic). Flexible benefit components such as leave travel allowance and
meal cards can be elected during the annual declaration window in April.

## How is provident fund (PF) handled?

Both the employee and Acme contribute 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund each
month. The employee contribution is deducted from salary. Employees can view their PF balance via
the EPFO member portal using their UAN, which is printed on every payslip.

## How is income tax deducted?

Tax is deducted at source (TDS) each month based on the employee's projected annual income and the
tax regime chosen in the April declaration. Employees may switch between the old and new regime
once per financial year, by 15 April. Investment proofs for the old regime must be uploaded to the
HR portal by 31 January; otherwise TDS is recomputed without the deductions in February and March.

## Who is eligible for overtime?

Employees in grades A1 to A3 (associate, senior associate, and analyst) are eligible for overtime
pay. Employees in grade B1 and above (team lead and higher) are exempt and are instead eligible for
compensatory off.

## How is overtime calculated?

Overtime applies to approved work beyond 9 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week. Overtime hours
are paid at twice the ordinary hourly rate, where the ordinary hourly rate is monthly basic salary
plus dearness allowance divided by 208. Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the reporting
manager and is capped at 50 hours per quarter.

## What is compensatory off?

Employees who work on a public holiday or weekend with manager approval are entitled to one
compensatory off day for each day worked. Comp-offs must be used within 60 days of being earned
and cannot be encashed.

## What is the night shift allowance?

Employees working an approved shift that includes at least 4 hours between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM
receive a night shift allowance of INR 500 per shift, paid with the following month's salary.

## How are reimbursements processed?

Expense claims submitted in the finance portal by the 20th of the month are reimbursed with that
month's salary. Claims submitted after the 20th are paid in the following month. Original receipts
are required for any claim above INR 500.

## When are annual increments and bonuses paid?

Annual increments take effect on 1 April and are reflected in the April salary paid at the end of
April. The annual performance bonus is paid in June, pro-rated for employees who joined after
1 April of the previous year. Employees serving a notice period on the bonus payment date are not
eligible for the bonus.

## How do I correct an error in my payslip?

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under Payroll > Payslip Query within 10 days of payday. Confirmed
errors are corrected in the next payroll cycle, or by an off-cycle payment if the shortfall exceeds
10% of net pay.

## What happens on my last working day?

Full and final settlement, including earned leave encashment and any pending reimbursements, is
paid within 45 days of the last working day. Form 16 for the financial year is issued by 15 June.
