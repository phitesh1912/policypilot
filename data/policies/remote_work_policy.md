# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Acme Corp supports remote and hybrid work. It applies to all employees
globally. Roles are classified as office-based, hybrid, or fully remote in the HR system; your
classification is agreed with your manager and shown in your profile.

## Hybrid work expectations

Hybrid employees are expected to work from their assigned office at least 2 days per week, with
Tuesday designated as a team anchor day. Managers may set an additional team anchor day. Office
attendance is tracked through badge access for space-planning purposes only.

## Fully remote work

Fully remote roles require approval from the department head and HR at the time of hire or
transfer. Fully remote employees must live in a country where Acme has a legal entity or an
employer-of-record arrangement, and are expected to visit their home office at least once per
quarter at Acme's expense.

## Working hours and availability

Employees are expected to be available during their team's core hours, which are 11:00 AM to
4:00 PM in the team's primary time zone. Outside core hours, employees may flex their schedule
with manager agreement. Employees should set their working hours in the calendar system and keep
their status up to date in the chat tool.

## Home office equipment

Acme provides every employee with a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset. Hybrid and
fully remote employees may additionally claim a one-time home office setup allowance of USD 750
(or INR 40,000) for a desk, chair, and lighting, within the first 90 days of becoming remote or
hybrid. Equipment purchased with the allowance remains the employee's property. Acme-issued
equipment must be returned on separation.

## Internet and utilities

Hybrid and fully remote employees receive a monthly internet stipend of USD 50 (or INR 2,000),
paid through payroll. Acme does not reimburse electricity or other utilities.

## Working from another location

Employees may work from a location other than their registered home address within the same
country for up to 4 weeks per calendar year without approval, provided they notify their manager.
Working from a different country requires written approval from HR at least 4 weeks in advance,
is limited to 30 days per calendar year, and may be refused for tax, immigration, or security
reasons.

## Security requirements

Remote employees must use the Acme VPN when accessing internal systems, enable full-disk encryption
and automatic screen lock on all devices, and must not use public or shared computers for Acme
work. Confidential documents must not be printed at home. Video calls involving customer data must
be taken in a private space.

## Health and safety

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe home workspace. Acme offers a free virtual
ergonomic assessment on request. Work-related injuries at home must be reported to HR within
24 hours, as they would be in the office.

## Changing your work arrangement

Requests to change between office-based, hybrid, and fully remote classifications are submitted
through the HR portal and require manager and HR approval. Acme may require a return to hybrid or
office-based work with 60 days' notice where business needs change.
