# Payroll & Overtime FAQ — United States

## When am I paid?

Acme US pays on a bi-weekly schedule, every other Friday, for the two-week period ending the
previous Saturday. There are 26 pay periods per year. If a payday falls on a bank holiday, pay is
deposited on the preceding business day. Pay statements are available in the HR portal on the
Wednesday before payday.

## Exempt vs non-exempt employees

Each role is classified under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) as exempt or non-exempt.
Non-exempt employees are paid hourly, must record all hours worked in the timekeeping system, and
are eligible for overtime. Exempt employees are paid a fixed salary and are not eligible for
overtime pay. Your classification is shown on your offer letter and in the HR portal.

## How is overtime calculated?

Non-exempt employees are paid 1.5 times their regular hourly rate for all hours worked over 40 in a
workweek. The Acme workweek runs Sunday 12:00 AM to Saturday 11:59 PM. Paid time off, sick leave,
and holidays do not count as hours worked for overtime purposes. In California, daily overtime
applies: 1.5 times for hours over 8 in a day and 2 times for hours over 12 in a day.

## Does overtime need approval?

Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Unapproved overtime will still be paid as
required by law, but repeated unapproved overtime may result in corrective action.

## What is the shift differential?

Non-exempt employees working an approved shift where the majority of hours fall between 6:00 PM and
6:00 AM receive a shift differential of USD 2.50 per hour. Weekend shifts receive USD 1.50 per hour.
Differentials are included in the regular rate when calculating overtime.

## How are taxes withheld?

Federal income tax is withheld based on your Form W-4. State and local taxes are withheld based on
your work and residence addresses in the HR portal. Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%)
are withheld as required. Update your W-4 in the HR portal at any time; changes take effect in the
next pay period.

## 401(k) contributions

Employees may contribute to the Acme 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. Acme matches 100% of
the first 4% of eligible pay contributed. Employer matching contributions vest 25% per year of
service and are fully vested after 4 years. Contribution changes made by the 15th take effect in
the next pay period.

## How are expense reimbursements paid?

Approved expense reports submitted by Wednesday are reimbursed on the following payday via direct
deposit. Receipts are required for any expense over USD 25.

## Annual raises and bonuses

Merit increases take effect on the first pay period in March. The annual bonus is paid on the
second payday in March for employees active on the payment date who were employed on or before
30 September of the prior year. Bonuses are subject to supplemental federal withholding at 22%.

## How do I report a pay error?

Submit a ticket in the HR portal under Payroll > Pay Inquiry. Confirmed underpayments are
corrected by off-cycle payment within 3 business days. Overpayments are recovered from the next
regular paycheck unless a repayment plan is agreed.

## Final pay on separation

Final pay is issued in accordance with state law. In states without a specific requirement, final
pay is issued on the next regular payday. Accrued PTO is paid out where required by state law or
policy. Your W-2 is mailed and posted to the HR portal by 31 January.
