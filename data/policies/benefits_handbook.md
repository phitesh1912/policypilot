# Benefits Handbook

This handbook summarises the benefits available to Acme Corp employees globally. Region-specific
plan details, insurers, and statutory benefits are published in the India and United States
addenda in the HR portal.

## Health insurance

All full-time employees are enrolled in Acme's group health insurance plan from their date of
joining. Coverage includes the employee, spouse or domestic partner, and dependent children up to
age 25. Parents may be added as dependents at the employee's cost during the annual enrolment
window. In India, the base sum insured is INR 500,000 per family per year with an option to top up
to INR 1,500,000. In the United States, employees choose between a PPO plan and a high-deductible
plan with a health savings account; Acme contributes USD 1,000 per year to the HSA.

## Dental and vision

Dental and vision coverage is available as an optional add-on in all regions. Premiums are deducted
from salary monthly.

## Life and disability insurance

Acme provides group term life insurance equal to 3 times annual base salary, at no cost to the
employee. Long-term disability insurance replaces 60% of base salary after a 90-day waiting period.
Employees may purchase additional voluntary life cover up to 5 times base salary.

## Employee assistance programme (EAP)

All employees and their household members have access to a confidential employee assistance
programme offering up to 8 free counselling sessions per issue per year, available 24/7 by phone or
video. The EAP is provided by an independent third party, and Acme does not receive any information
about who uses the service.

## Wellness allowance

Employees receive a wellness allowance of USD 500 (or INR 30,000) per calendar year, reimbursable
for gym memberships, fitness equipment, sports classes, meditation apps, and ergonomic home-office
furniture. Claims are submitted via the expense portal with receipts. Unused allowance does not
carry over.

## Learning and development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,500 (or INR 90,000) for courses,
certifications, conferences, and books related to their role or career growth. Requests above
USD 500 require manager approval before purchase. Acme also reimburses one professional
certification exam fee per year, including one retake.

## Retirement savings

In India, Acme contributes to the Employees' Provident Fund as described in the Payroll FAQ, and
employees may additionally enrol in the National Pension System through salary deduction. In the
United States, the 401(k) plan is described in the Payroll FAQ.

## Employee stock purchase plan (ESPP)

Employees with at least 6 months of service may enrol in the ESPP during the May and November
enrolment windows. Participants contribute 1% to 10% of base salary and purchase Acme shares at a
15% discount to the lower of the price at the start or end of each 6-month offering period.

## Commuter and remote-work benefits

Office-based employees in the US may set aside pre-tax dollars for transit and parking up to the
IRS limit. Employees in India receive a transport allowance as part of their CTC. Remote-work
equipment benefits are described in the Remote Work Policy.

## Referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and completes 90 days of employment receive a referral
bonus of USD 2,000 (or INR 50,000) for engineering roles and USD 1,000 (or INR 25,000) for other
roles. Referral bonuses are paid with the next regular payroll after the 90-day mark and are
taxable.

## Enrolment and changes

The annual benefits enrolment window is 1–15 November for the following calendar year. Outside the
window, changes are permitted only within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage,
birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage.
